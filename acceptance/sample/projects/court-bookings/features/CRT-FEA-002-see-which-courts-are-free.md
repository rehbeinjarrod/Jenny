# CRT-FEA-002 · See which courts are free

Code: CRT
Feature: CRT-FEA-002

Anyone can see, hour by hour, which courts are free over the next two weeks before they book one.

## Requirements

- **CRT-REQ-005** The timetable shows, for each of the four courts, which hours between 7 am and 10 pm are free over the next 14 days. · Source: CRT-SRC-003 · Status: active
- **CRT-REQ-006** Anyone can see the timetable without signing in. · Source: CRT-SRC-003 · Status: active
- **CRT-REQ-007** A booked hour shows only as taken, never who booked it. · Source: CRT-SRC-003 · Status: active
- **CRT-REQ-008** An hour shows as taken as soon as its booking is paid. · Source: CRT-SRC-003 · Status: active
