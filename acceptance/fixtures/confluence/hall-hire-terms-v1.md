# Hall hire terms

These terms apply to every hire of the Riverbend community hall. The committee reviews them each year.

## Booking

A booking is confirmed once the deposit has been paid.

Every booking must say how many guests are expected. The hall holds no more than 120 guests, so a booking for more than 120 cannot be made.

Hirers agree to these terms online before they pay the deposit.

## Setting up

If the hirer asks when booking, the caretaker sets out the tables and chairs before the booking starts, for a $20 fee.

## Contact

Anything unclear in these terms? Email the hall committee at committee@riverbend-hall.example.
