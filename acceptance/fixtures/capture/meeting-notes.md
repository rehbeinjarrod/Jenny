Hall committee meeting, 10 November 2026
Present: Margaret Osei (chair), Dana Wells, Ravi Kumar

1. Booking ahead. Ravi said the old paper diary let anyone book six months out, and nobody had complained. The committee was happy with that: non-members can book up to six months ahead, the same as members.

2. When we cancel on a hirer. Ravi read out section 3.4 of the Community Halls Association's Hire Venues Code of Practice (2024): "Where a venue withdraws a confirmed hire for any reason within its control, the hirer shall receive every sum paid towards that hire, returned by the original method of payment within seven days." The committee agreed to follow the Code: if we cancel a booking, the hirer gets the whole deposit back within seven days.

3. Any other business. None.
