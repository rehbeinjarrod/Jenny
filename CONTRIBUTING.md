# Contributing

**Jenny is open source, but not open to contributions.**

The maintainer does not accept code, documentation, skill or other
contributions, and does not take feature requests. Issues and pull requests
are turned off for this repository, so neither can be opened; a patch that
reaches the maintainer any other way may be ignored without review. This is
not a judgement of the work. It keeps the project small enough for one person
to look after in their own time, with all of its content from a single author.

You are welcome to fork the project and change it however you like, under
the terms of the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).

For support, see [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). In short: there isn't any.
